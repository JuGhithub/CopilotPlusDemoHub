AIR HANDLING UNIT MAINTENANCE GUIDE

SECTION 1 - FAN VIBRATION

Excessive fan vibration may be caused by:

- Bearing wear
- Shaft misalignment
- Blade damage
- Loose mounting hardware
- Airflow obstruction

Recommended inspection:

1. Inspect fan blades.
2. Verify shaft alignment.
3. Inspect bearings.
4. Check mounting bolts.
5. Validate airflow path.

SECTION 2 - MOTOR OVERHEATING

Motor overheating may be caused by:

- Restricted airflow
- Electrical imbalance
- Bearing friction
- Excessive load

If motor temperature exceeds the approved operating range:

1. Stop the unit.
2. Inspect airflow conditions.
3. Check motor current.
4. Verify cooling airflow.

SECTION 3 - BEARING INSPECTION

Inspect bearings every six months.

Signs of bearing degradation:

- Abnormal noise
- Excessive vibration
- Increased temperature
- Lubrication leakage

Replace bearings showing significant wear.
